  1. Understand your requirements: We identify your space, lifestyle needs, preferences and priorities.
  2. Plan the layout: We consider room dimensions, furniture placement, movement and storage.
  3. Select materials and finishes: We consider practical materials, finishes and design choices that suit the overall space.
  4. Coordinate implementation: The planned design is organized into a practical implementation approach.
