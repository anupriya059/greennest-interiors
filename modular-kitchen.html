Modular Kitchen Layouts

The right kitchen layout depends on the available space, room dimensions and how the kitchen is used. Common layouts can include:

L-Shaped Kitchen

An L-shaped layout can make efficient use of two connected walls while providing practical work and storage areas.

Parallel Kitchen

A parallel layout uses two opposing sides and can provide separate preparation, cooking and storage zones.

Space-Efficient Kitchen

Smaller kitchens can be planned with carefully selected storage and layouts that make everyday movement easier.

What We Consider in Kitchen Design

  • Storage requirements: Planning cabinets and storage based on everyday kitchen needs.
  • Workflow: Organizing preparation, cooking and cleaning areas for convenient use.
  • Lighting: Considering practical lighting for preparation and everyday activities.
  • Materials and finishes: Selecting suitable materials and finishes based on the desired appearance and practical requirements.
  • Available space: Planning the kitchen around the room dimensions and existing layout.
