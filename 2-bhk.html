2 BHK Interior Design in Chennai

GreenNest Interiors helps Chennai homeowners plan practical and comfortable interiors for 2 BHK homes. Our approach focuses on making the best use of available space without making the home feel crowded.

We combine functional layouts, useful storage, comfortable movement and consistent design elements to create a home that works well for everyday living.

Areas We Plan

Living Room

Plan furniture placement, storage and movement to create a comfortable and welcoming living space.

Kitchen

Improve kitchen organization with practical layouts and storage solutions suited to the available space.

Bedrooms

Create comfortable bedrooms with thoughtful furniture placement and storage planning.

Wardrobes and Storage

Use practical storage solutions to keep everyday belongings organized while making efficient use of available space.

Key Design Priorities for a 2 BHK Home

  • Efficient storage: Make better use of available space with practical storage solutions.
  • Comfortable circulation: Maintain clear and convenient movement
